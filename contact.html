Email us

For questions, bug reports, billing help, or privacy requests, write to support@quillstrike.com. We usually reply within one to two business days.

What to include

  • The email address you signed up with, so we can find your account.
  • What you were doing and what went wrong, with a screenshot if it helps.
  • For billing questions, the date and amount of the charge.

Privacy and account requests

To export or delete your data, or to disconnect a broker connection, you can do it yourself in Settings and Import, or email us and we’ll take care of it. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
