Revenge trading: how to spot it in your own data

6 min read · Updated November 3, 2026

Revenge trading is jumping back into the market right after a loss to “win it back.” Almost every trader does it at some point, and it’s hard to see in the moment. Your trade history, though, shows it clearly.

What it looks like

  • A new trade opened within minutes of closing a loser
  • A bigger position than usual right after a loss
  • A setup you wouldn’t normally take, or one outside your usual hours
  • Several losing trades clustered on the same day

How to find it in your journal

You don’t need to remember how you felt. Compare these groups using your own trades:

CompareWhat a revenge pattern looks like
Trades opened within an hour of a loss vs. all other tradesLower win rate and a worse average result after losses
Your next trade after a loss vs. after a winThe trade after a loss does noticeably worse
Position size after a loss vs. your normal sizeSize goes up right after losing
Days with 2 or more losses vs. other daysA large share of your total losses comes from a few bad days
Example: suppose trades opened within an hour of a loss averaged −$34 with a 30% win rate, while all your other trades averaged +$18 with a 62% win rate. That gap is the cost of the habit, in dollars.

Tag it when it happens

Numbers catch the pattern, but tags tell you why. Add a quick tag like “revenge” or “FOMO” whenever you notice it, even after the fact. After a few weeks, you can compare tagged trades with everything else.

Rules that are easy to measure

The most effective fixes are simple enough to check against every trade:

  • Cool-off period: no new trades for 30–60 minutes after a loss
  • Daily loss limit: stop for the day after a set dollar loss
  • Stop after a losing streak: no new trades after two losses in a row
  • No size increases after a loss: keep size fixed until your next win, or until tomorrow

Pick one, write it down, and check whether you followed it. A rule you measure is far more likely to stick than one you just try to remember.
